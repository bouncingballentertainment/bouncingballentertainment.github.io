YouClipping

YouClipping is a personal automation tool by Bouncing Ball Entertainment. It helps edit short vertical clips from videos whose creators have given permission, and uploads the finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The tool is not a public service. It has a single user, the channel owner, who signs in with their own Google account to authorize uploads and to read the performance statistics of their own clips.
